Important for Reception Desks: If your clinic collects consultation fees upfront at check-in, ensure the Receptionist role has both View invoices and payments and Record a payment switched on. Otherwise, the bill will generate but the desk cannot record the money.

3. First-Time Setup (Settings › Billing)

Before issuing real bills, configure clinic billing parameters once under Settings › Billing:

1. Payment PolicyPay in full / Part / Credit

2. Accepted MethodsCash, UPI, Card, Bank

3. Tax & Printed BillGST %, Terms, Footer note
 →
4. Document SeriesINV, RCP, RFD per branch
  1. Payment Policy: Choose Pay in full (recommended for standard OPD), Part payment allowed (for procedures/instalments), or Pay later (credit) (allows registered patients to carry balances).
  2. Payment Methods: Enable the payment modes accepted at your counter: Cash, UPI, Credit/Debit Card, Bank Transfer, Cheque.
  3. Document Number Series: Customize official numbering prefixes per branch (e.g. GKP/INV/26-27/00001 for invoices, GKP/RCP/26-27/00001 for receipts, GKP/RFD/26-27/00001 for refund vouchers). All sequences automatically restart each financial year on 1 April.
  4. Department Timing: Specify when OPD bills trigger (e.g. After consultation) and when Lab bills trigger (e.g. When tests ordered).
  5. Services & Pricing: In Billing › Services & Prices and Doctor profiles, set doctor consultation fees, follow-up rules, registration fees, and nursing/procedure charges.

Middle Analytics Row

  • Revenue Trend: Multi-series bar and line chart displaying Billed revenue (blue bars) against Collections (green line) across customizable daily, weekly, or monthly timeframes. Hovering reveals day-by-day totals.
  • Collection by Service: Donut chart showing exact revenue distribution across clinic service lines: Consultation (OPD), Lab Tests, Pharmacy, Procedures, and Others, complete with percentage share and rupee values.
  • Payment Status: Donut breakdown of invoice states: Paid, Part Paid, and Unpaid.

Invoices Register & Quick Tabs

Directly beneath the analytics cards is the interactive invoice ledger with 5 filter tabs:

  • All Invoices: Complete chronological register of all invoices raised in the selected date window.
  • Outstanding: Only invoices with unpaid balances requiring cashier follow-up.
  • Open Visits: Draft bills still collecting procedure or consultation charges while the patient is in the clinic.
  • Paid: Fully cleared invoices with zero balance.
  • Cancelled: Invoices voided before payment or fully refunded.

Split Tender Example

A bill of ₹1,200 is settled with ₹500 in cash and ₹700 via GooglePay UPI. The cashier adds both lines in the payment popup. The system generates one unified receipt GKP/RCP/26-27/00045 documenting both tenders for auditing.

Double-Click Protection: The payment dialog generates a unique transaction signature. If a cashier clicks "Record Payment" twice or network flickers, the system detects the signature and executes the transaction only once. Money is never double-charged.

Aging Filter & Overdue Days

Staff can filter outstanding accounts by age to prioritize recovery:

  • 0–30 Days: Current month visits. Normal grace period.
  • 31–60 Days: Follow-up required via WhatsApp reminder or phone call.
  • 61–90 Days: Overdue balances. Requires manager intervention before scheduling new appointments.
  • 90+ Days: Delinquent balances for audit or write-off review.

Collecting an Outstanding Balance

  1. Navigate to Billing › Outstanding.
  2. Locate the patient's record by searching name, phone number, or invoice number.
  3. Click the blue Take payment button directly on the row.
  4. Enter the collected amount, select payment method, and submit. The invoice status instantly updates to Paid when fully cleared.

10. Pharmacy Counter Billing & Returns

The pharmacy operates on dedicated POS sales counters with automatic inventory batch allocation:

  • FIFO Batch Allocation: When medicines are added to a cart, HMS automatically assigns the earliest expiring batch that is in stock.
  • Credit Sales to Registered Patients: Regular registered patients can be extended credit if enabled in Pharmacy › Settings. Walk-in buyers must always pay in full.
  • Medicine Returns: When a patient returns unopened strips/syrups, click Take a return on the pharmacy bill. The returned units automatically increment the correct batch inventory, and a refund or balance credit is issued.

12. Troubleshooting Guide

Symptom / MessageRoot CauseAction to Fix
"This clinic takes bills in full"Clinic setting is set to Pay in Full, but cashier entered partial amount.Collect remaining balance or split across methods (e.g. Cash + UPI).
No "Take payment" button visibleInvoice is already 100% paid, or logged-in user lacks Record payment permission.Check invoice status or ask Admin to update user role permissions.
Doctor fee not appearing on check-inDoctor profile has ₹0 or empty consultation fee, or billing timing is set to Post-consultation.Update doctor profile fees in Doctors list.
Patient visit stays "Payment Pending"One department bill (OPD, Lab, or Pharmacy) still carries an outstanding balance.Open Billing › Outstanding to identify and clear the unpaid bill.
Cannot edit invoice itemsA receipt has already been generated against this invoice.Issue a refund voucher first, or cancel after full refund.
