Billing and Payments: User Guide

HMS · For reception, cashiers, doctors, pharmacists, lab staff, managers and owners · October 2026

In one line: the OPD, the lab and the pharmacy each make their own bill and take their own money. Medicines are never added to the doctor's bill, and tests are never added to the doctor's fee. Owners and managers see all the money in one place, Billing › Finance, where every rupee is counted once.

1. The three bills of a visit

One patient visit can create up to three separate bills. Each one is paid at its own counter. Because they are separate, nothing is ever charged twice.

How to change a role

  1. Open Roles & Permissions from the sidebar.
  2. Click the role on the left, for example Receptionist.
  3. Open the Permissions tab and click a group, for example Billing & Payments.
  4. Switch each permission on or off.
  5. Click Save Changes.

The Editing box above the tabs says where the change applies. Leave it on The whole organisation to change every branch. Pick one branch to switch a permission off at that branch only. A branch can only take a permission away; it can never add one.

Check your receptionist. If your clinic bills at check-in, the receptionist must also have View invoices and payments and Record a payment. Otherwise the bill is made but nobody at the desk can take the money. Organisations set up before October 2026 should check this once.
The owner can do almost everything, but cannot check patients in. That is the desk's job, so the owner needs a staff login for it.

1. Payment ruleSettings › Billing › PaymentsPay in full, Part payment allowed or Pay later (credit). See section 5.
2. Payment methodsSettings › Billing › PaymentsTick every method you accept. The pharmacy uses the same list. "Credit" is not a method; it is the payment rule above.
3. Tax and printed billSettings › Billing › Tax, Printed billWhether prices already include tax, the default tax rate, and the terms and footer printed on every bill. A change affects new bills only.
4. Bill numbersSettings › Billing › Per branchEach branch numbers its own bills, receipts, refunds, pharmacy bills and pharmacy returns, for example GGN/INV/26-27/00001. Numbers restart every 1 April. Bills already issued keep their numbers.
5. OPD timingSettings › Billing › Billing by department › OPDWhen the patient checks in (fee taken first), After the consultation (most common), or Only when the desk bills it.
6. Lab timingSettings › Billing › Billing by department › LaboratoryWhen the tests are ordered, After the lab signs off, or Only when the lab counter bills it.
7. PricesDoctors (each doctor's profile) and Billing › Services & pricesSet each doctor's consultation fee. Add services such as a registration charge, a follow-up fee or a dressing. A branch can have its own price for a service.
8. RolesRoles & PermissionsMake sure the people at the billing desk can view bills and record payments (section 2).
Pharmacy settings (price basis, tax, walk-in sales, credit sales, prescription check) are in Pharmacy › Settings. The Pharmacy card on the Billing settings page shows a summary and a link.

What goes on the OPD bill

  • The doctor's fee — the "default consultation fee" on the doctor's profile.
  • A follow-up fee instead — if the patient comes back to the same doctor within the follow-up days the doctor wrote last time, and you have a "Follow-up fee" service. Set its price to 0 to make follow-ups free.
  • "Per visit" and "Procedure" services — added to every visit automatically. If a branch has its own price for a service, only the branch price is used.

Example. Dr. Mehta's fee is ₹500 and the clinic has a ₹100 "Registration" per-visit service. Every new visit to Dr. Mehta makes one bill of ₹600: GGN/INV/26-27/00042.

One visit has only one OPD bill. Checking in again, reopening the consultation or pressing Bill visit twice brings back the same bill. It never makes a second one.

The three payment rules

RuleWhat happens at the deskGood for
Pay in full (default)The whole amount must be paid in one go. The patient can still pay with two methods at once using Split across another method.Most clinics
Part payment allowedThe patient can pay some now and the rest later. The visit shows "Payment pending" until it is cleared.Clinics that take instalments
Pay later (credit)A registered patient can leave without paying. The visit closes and the amount waits in Billing › Outstanding. Walk-in patients cannot use credit.Regular patients with an account
Clicking twice is safe. Each payment window has its own hidden ID. If the button is clicked twice, or the internet drops and the request is sent again, the second click returns the first payment. Money is never recorded twice.

How to give a refund

  1. Open the bill.
  2. In the Payments list, click Refund next to the payment you are giving back.
  3. Type the amount and the reason, then click Refund.
  4. A refund slip is made, for example GGN/RFD/26-27/00003. Print it from the same list.

A refund lowers the bill. The patient is not asked for that money again.

ExampleStatus afterStill owed
₹600 bill, ₹600 paid, ₹100 refunded as a discountPart refunded₹0
₹600 bill, ₹600 paid, ₹600 refundedRefunded₹0
Nothing is ever deleted. A cancelled bill, a refund or a correction stays on the record with who did it and when.

Opening a pharmacy bill

Go to Pharmacy › Bills, search by bill number, name or phone, and click View. The bill shows:

  • The patient — name, patient ID, age, gender, phone, town, and Open patient file. A walk-in shows a "Walk-in" tag.
  • The bill — date and time, store, who billed it, the doctor (if it came from a prescription), and the payment status.
  • The items — with "10 back" under any quantity that was returned.
  • Money in and out — every payment, refund and return, next to the totals. "Owed" is red; "Settled" is green.

Collecting a balance later

  1. Open the bill in Pharmacy › Bills.
  2. Click Take payment · ₹… at the bottom.
  3. Choose the method, check the amount, and click Take payment.

Example. A patient paid ₹84 by card for 30 Dolo 650 tablets and one ORS packet. They bring back 10 tablets. The return is worth ₹20.70, so ₹20.70 goes back to the card. The return gets its own number, GGN/PHR/26-27/00001, and the bill shows "10 back".

Cancel this bill puts every item back on the shelf and records any money taken as a refund. The bill stays on the record, marked Cancelled.

9. The Finance screen

Billing › Finance shows the money of the OPD, the lab and the pharmacy together. Pick the dates at the top right. A branch manager sees their own branch; head office sees every branch.

FigureWhat it means
BilledThe total of the bills raised in the dates you picked. Cancelled bills are not counted.
CollectedThe money actually received in those dates, from payments only.
RefundedThe money given back in those dates.
Net collectionsCollected minus refunded — what really stayed with you.
OutstandingWhat patients owe today, whenever the bill was made.

Below the figures you see the same numbers by department, by payment method, by branch and day by day, and one list of every bill from all three departments. Switch on Only bills still owed to see who has to pay.

Example. In October the OPD billed ₹1,750 that is still unpaid and the pharmacy has ₹13,575 on credit. Outstanding shows ₹15,325, and the department table shows the two parts separately.

10. When something does not work

What you seeWhy, and what to do
"This clinic takes bills in full"The payment rule is Pay in full. Take the whole amount (split it across methods if needed), or ask the owner to choose Part payment allowed.
No Take payment buttonYour role does not have Record a payment, or the bill is already paid or cancelled. Ask the owner to check your role (section 2).
No bill after check-inCheck the OPD timing in Settings › Billing. Check that the doctor has a consultation fee. Check that billing is switched on for that branch.
The visit stays "Payment pending"A bill for this visit still owes money: the OPD bill, a lab bill or a pharmacy bill. Clear it, or use the Pay later rule for registered patients.
"has had money taken against it, so it cannot be edited"A paid bill is never changed. Refund the payment, or refund all of it and cancel the bill, then make a new one.
"This clinic does not take …"That payment method is not ticked in Settings › Billing › Payments.
No Finance in the menuYour role needs View finance across OPD, lab and pharmacy.
A pharmacy sale will not save with money left unpaidCredit sales are off in Pharmacy › Settings, or the buyer is a walk-in. Take the full amount or choose a registered patient.
I clicked Record payment twiceNothing to do. The second click returned the first payment; there is only one receipt.
