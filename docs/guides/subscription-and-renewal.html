Subscription aur Renewal

ZEVRO · Aapke clinic ka plan · bache din, grace, lock aur renew · 16 October 2026
Ek line mein: aapka clinic ek plan par chalta hai jiski ek expiry date hoti hai. Expiry ke baad kuch din ka grace period milta hai. Grace bhi khatam ho jaaye to workspace lock ho jaata hai. Data kabhi delete nahi hota: renew karte hi sab wapas khul jaata hai.

1. Apna plan kahan dekhein

Owner (aur billing contact) ko Dashboard par sabse upar subscription ka card dikhta hai: plan ka naam, status, kitne din bache hain, expiry date, agla renewal, aur Renew now button.

Poori jaankari sidebar mein Organisation › Subscription par milti hai: capacity, is mahine ka usage, modules, add-ons, invoices aur usage history.

2. Bache hue din kaise gine jaate hain

Din calendar ke hisaab se, aapke clinic ke timezone (India mein IST) mein gine jaate hain. Subscription expiry date ki raat 11:59 tak chalta hai.

Card par likha haiMatlab
7 days leftAaj ke baad 7 poore din aur bache hain.
1 day leftKal raat 11:59 tak chalega.
0 days left / ends todayAaj raat 11:59 par term khatam ho jaayega.

Expiry ke kuch din pehle (aam taur par 7, 3 aur 1 din) owner ko email aata hai, aur har page ke upar ek peeli patti dikhti hai.

3. Expiry, grace aur lock

HaalatKya hota haiAapko kya karna hai
ActiveSab kuch normal chalta hai.Kuch nahi.
Expiring soonSab chalta hai; card aur har page par chetavni.Renew kar dein.
Grace periodTerm khatam ho gaya, par kuch din aur kaam chalta rehta hai. (Platform ne grace ko read-only rakha ho to sirf dekh sakte hain, naya kuch save nahi hota.)Jaldi renew karein. Grace ke din card par dikhte hain.
Locked / ExpiredWorkspace band. Login ke baad "Subscription expired" page dikhta hai. Appointment, bill, patient, sab ruk jaata hai. Data delete nahi hota.Owner renew kare. Payment hote hi sab turant khul jaata hai.
SuspendedPlatform ne billing ki wajah se (jaise cheque bounce) account rok diya.Hum se sampark karein. Sirf payment karne se ye nahi khulta.
Lock ka faisla server har request par expiry date dekhkar karta hai. Isliye app ke kisi button se, ya seedhe API URL se bhi, locked workspace mein kaam nahi ho sakta.

Lock rehte hue bhi ye khula rehta hai: sign out, apni profile, Guide, aur Subscription page (taaki renew kar sakein). Patients ki app mein patient apne purane prescriptions aur reports dekh sakte hain, par naya booking ya message nahi kar sakte.

4. Renew aur upgrade kaise karein

  1. Subscription page par Renew dabayein.
  2. Plan aur billing (Monthly / Annual) chunein. Wahi plan pehle se chuna hota hai.
  3. Continue to payment: invoice banta hai (GST ke saath) aur Razorpay ka payment window khulta hai.
  4. Payment hote hi subscription renew ho jaata hai, aur lock tha to turant khul jaata hai.

Jaldi renew kiya? Koi din bekar nahi jaata: naya term purane term ke khatam hone ke baad se shuru hota hai.

Upgrade (bada plan) turant lagta hai, aur bache hue dinon ka sirf farak dena hota hai. Chhota plan agle renewal se lagta hai: renew karte waqt chunein, ya hum se kahein.

Price badla? Chalte term mein aapka tay daam nahi badalta. Naya daam agle renewal se lagta hai, aur renewal invoice expiry se pehle hi bhej diya jaata hai.

Online payment set na ho to invoice ban jaata hai aur bank transfer ki jaankari dikhti hai. Hum payment record karte hi subscription renew kar dete hain.

5. Limits aur monthly allowance

CheezKaise gini jaati hai
BranchesActive branches. Band (inactive) ya delete ki hui branch nahi gini jaati.
DoctorsHar doctor ek baar, chahe woh kitni bhi branches mein baithte hon. Inactive doctor nahi gina jaata.
EmployeesActive staff logins. Owner, patients ke logins aur doctor ka apna login nahi gina jaata.
WhatsApp / Email / Video minutesHar calendar month ka allowance. Mahina badalte hi naya allowance.

Limit poori ho jaaye to naya add karne par saaf message aata hai ("Your plan allows 10 doctors..."). Purana record nahi hatta. Kisi ko inactive karke jagah khali kar sakte hain, ya add-on khareed sakte hain.

WhatsApp/email ka allowance khatam ho jaaye to message nahi jaata, aur Communication log mein "failed" ke saath wajah likhi hoti hai. Kuch plans mein allowance ke baad bhi message jaate hain aur agle invoice mein chhota charge lagta hai. Video call mein minute jawab dene se phone rakhne tak gine jaate hain (poore minute mein, upar ki taraf).

Same message ke retry ya do baar aaye delivery update par do baar charge nahi lagta. Jo message fail ho jaaye, uska charge wapas ho jaata hai.

6. Add-ons kharidna

Subscription › Buy add-ons: extra branch, doctor ya staff ki jagah, WhatsApp/email ke credit packs, ya koi module (jaise Laboratory). Quantity chunte hi poora daam (GST ke saath) dikhta hai. Recurring add-on is term ke bache dinon ke hisaab se lagta hai aur phir plan ke saath renew hota hai. Credit pack sirf is mahine ke liye hota hai.

7. Invoice aur payment

Har renewal, upgrade, add-on aur allowance ke upar ke usage ka invoice Subscription › Invoices & payments mein milta hai. Jo invoice abhi baaki ho, uske aage Pay button hota hai.

Payment tabhi maana jaata hai jab server Razorpay se khud confirm kar le. Browser band ho gaya ho tab bhi Razorpay ki khabar (webhook) se payment record ho jaata hai.

8. Kisko kya dikhta hai

KaunKya
OwnerDashboard ka card, Subscription page, renew/upgrade/add-ons.
Billing contactJis role mein "View and renew the subscription, and buy add-ons" ho. Owner ise role mein de sakta hai.
Baaki staffSirf chetavni ki patti, aur lock hone par "owner se renew karwayein" ka message.
