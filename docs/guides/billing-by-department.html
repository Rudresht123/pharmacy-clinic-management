Billing: Har Department ka Apna Bill

HMS · Billing, Lab, Pharmacy aur Finance · ye kaise kaam karta hai · October 2026

Ek line mein: OPD, Lab aur Pharmacy teeno apna-apna bill banate hain aur apne counter par paisa lete hain. Dawai ka paisa kabhi OPD ke bill mein nahi judta, aur test ka paisa kabhi doctor ki fees ke saath nahi. Malik aur manager sab kuch ek jagah Billing › Finance par dekhte hain, jahan har rupaya sirf ek baar gina jaata hai.

OPDDoctor ki fees (ya follow-up fees), "Per visit" aur "Procedure" wali servicesSettings ke hisaab se: check-in par, consultation ke baad, ya sirf jab desk banayeBilling counter (Take payment)
LabUs order ke priced tests, order ke din ki keemat parTest order hote hi, lab sign-off ke baad, ya sirf haath seBilling › Invoices (Laboratory)

Ek visit mein teen bill ho sakte hain: OPD ka, har lab order ka ek, aur pharmacy ka. Teeno alag hain, isliye koi cheez do baar charge nahi hoti.

3. OPD billing

Bill mein kya judta hai

  • Doctor ki fees — doctor ke profile ki "default consultation fee".
  • Follow-up fees — agar patient usi doctor ke paas, pichhli visit mein doctor ke likhe follow-up dinon ke andar wapas aaya, aur price list mein "Follow-up fee" service hai, to doctor ki fees ki jagah yeh lagti hai. Isko 0 rakhein to follow-up free.
  • "Per visit" aur "Procedure" services — har visit ke bill mein apne aap. Agar branch ki apni keemat hai to sirf wahi lagti hai, organisation wali saath mein nahi.

Kab banta hai

SettingKya hota hai
When the patient checks inCheck-in hote hi bill. Fees pehle lene wali clinic ke liye.
After the consultationDoctor "Complete" kare tab bill. Sabse aam.
Only when the desk bills itKuch apne aap nahi. OPD › Today par khatam hui visit ke saamne Bill visit button aata hai (sirf jinke paas bill banane ki permission hai).

Ek visit ka ek hi OPD bill. Consultation dobara khole aur complete kare, ya Bill visit do baar dabaye — wahi bill wapas milta hai. Bill bante hi "ready to pay" hota hai; ab koi bill draft mein atka nahi rehta.

4. Lab billing

Har lab order ka apna bill (Billing › Invoices mein "Laboratory" likha hota hai). Purani organisations ke liye default "Only when the lab counter bills it" hai — jab tak aap nahi chunte, kuch apne aap bill nahi hota.

  • When the tests are ordered — order bante hi bill; sample se pehle paisa.
  • After the lab signs off — result complete hone par bill.
  • Order mein test badle ya ek test cancel hua: jab tak paisa nahi liya, bill apne aap order ke saath badal jaata hai.
  • Poora order cancel: bina paise wala bill bhi cancel. Agar paisa le liya tha to bill khada rehta hai — paisa wapas dene ke liye refund karein, taaki record rahe.
  • Lab module band ho jaaye to naya lab bill nahi banta, lekin purane lab bill Billing aur Finance mein dikhte rehte hain.

5. Pharmacy counter

  • Sale ke saath hi paisa counter par. OPD patient ki dawai bhi wahin — kabhi OPD bill mein nahi.
  • Payment methods wahi jo Settings › Billing mein chalu hain. "Credit" koi method nahi hai.
  • Credit (udhaar) pharmacy settings ka "credit sales" switch decide karta hai, aur sirf registered customer ke liye.
  • Baad mein paisa lena: Pharmacy › Bills → bill kholein → Take payment.
  • Return: bill kholein → Take a return → kitna wapas aaya likhein. Stock usi batch mein wapas jaata hai. Paisa sirf utna wapas jitna naye total se zyada diya gaya tha; udhaar wale bill par baaki raqam kam ho jaati hai. Har return ka apna number, jaise GGN/PHR/26-27/00001.
  • Cancel: stock wapas, aur liya hua paisa "refund" row ke roop mein record hota hai.
  • Naye pharmacy bill branch aur saal ke hisaab se: GGN/PH/26-27/00001. Purane bill apne purane number (jaise INV-00042) ke saath rehte hain.

6. Payment ke niyam

SettingCounter par kya hota hai
Pay in full (default)Ek baar mein poora bakaya lena padta hai. Patient do tarah se de (jaise ₹300 cash + ₹200 UPI) to Take payment mein Split across another method se dono ek saath daalein.
Part payment allowedKuch abhi, baaki baad mein. Visit tab tak "Payment pending" rehti hai.
Pay later (credit)Registered patient bina poora paise diye ja sakta hai; visit band ho jaati hai aur raqam Outstanding mein dikhti hai. Walk-in ke liye credit nahi.
Dhyan dein: "Pay in full" pehle se default tha lekin lagu nahi hota tha. Ab lagu hota hai. Agar aapki clinic kishton mein paisa leti hai, to Settings › Billing mein "Part payment allowed" chunein.

Double click se do baar paisa nahi kat-ta. Payment, refund, pharmacy collection aur return — har window apni ek pehchaan (key) bhejti hai; dobara click par wahi pehli entry wapas milti hai.

7. Refund aur bill theek karna

Refund bill ko credit karta hai. Matlab jo paisa wapas diya, woh dobara maanga nahi jaata. ₹500 ke bill par ₹500 liya aur ₹500 wapas diya: bill Refunded, bakaya 0. ₹200 wapas diya: Part refunded, bakaya 0.

Bill ki haalatKya badal sakte hain
Draft (purana, band nahi hua)Lines theek ho sakti hain
Bana hua, koi paisa nahi liyaSirf agar Settings mein "Let staff correct a bill until money is taken" chalu hai
Kuch bhi paisa liya gaya (refund ke baad bhi)Kabhi nahi. Refund karein, ya cancel karke naya bill banayein — dono ka record rehta hai

Haath se banaye bill mein sirf service, procedure ya likhi hui line ja sakti hai — koi "pharmacy" ya "consultation" line haath se nahi. Har badlaav (pehle aur baad ki lines) activity log mein jaata hai.

Har bill par uski currency aur tax ka tareeka (tax shamil ya upar se) bante waqt likh diya jaata hai. Settings badalne se purana bill nahi badalta.

8. Finance screen

Billing › Finance (permission: "View finance across OPD, lab and pharmacy"). Paanch numbers, kabhi ek doosre ki jagah nahi:

NumberMatlab
BilledChune hue samay mein jitne ke bill bane (draft aur cancelled nahi)
CollectedJitna paisa sach mein liya gaya, jis din liya gaya
RefundedJitna wapas diya gaya
Net collectionsCollected minus Refunded
OutstandingAaj kitna baaki hai, bill kabhi bhi bana ho

Department, branch, payment method aur din/mahine ke hisaab se bhi. Neeche sabhi departments ke bill ek list mein. Paisa sirf payment ki entries se gina jaata hai, isliye koi payment do baar nahi judti. Branch manager sirf apni branch dekhta hai.
